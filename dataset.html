BREE-Dataset

Standardized Bimanual Manipulation Datasets

BREE-Dataset provides a unified collection of 8 bimanual manipulation tasks with expert demonstrations, all recorded on standardized hardware to ensure zero cross-instance drift. Every dataset includes synchronized camera streams, robot states, and actions, making it ready for immediate use in policy training and cross-platform transfer.

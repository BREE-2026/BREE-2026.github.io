Hardware Assembly 硬件装配

This page covers the workspace layout, arm placement with fixed USB assignment, the three-camera rig, and AprilTag placement. Careful, consistent assembly is what makes every BREE site identical. 本页介绍工作区布局、机械臂的固定 USB 分配摆放、三相机机架，以及 AprilTag 标签的放置。认真一致的装配是所有 BREE 站点完全一致的基础。

Note说明

Assembly photos and dimensioned drawings are being prepared and will be added to this page. The saved reference images in System Calibration already pin down the camera geometry — if your live view matches the reference, your rig is correctly assembled. 装配照片与尺寸图正在准备中，稍后补充到本页。系统标定中保存的参考画面已经锁定了相机几何——只要实时画面与参考一致，就说明机架装配正确。

1 · Workspace layout1 · 工作区布局

  • A fixed tabletop with a plain, consistent background; do not change lighting between sessions. 使用固定桌面与纯色一致的背景；会话之间不要改变光照。
  • The two follower arms are clamped to the table edge, facing each other across the shared workspace. 两条从臂固定夹在桌沿，隔着共享工作区相对而立。
  • The two leader arms stand beside the operator for two-handed teleoperation. 两条主臂放在操作者旁边，便于双手遥操作。

2 · Arm placement & USB port assignment2 · 机械臂摆放与 USB 端口分配

BREE fixes which arm lives on which serial port. Plug the arms into the host in the order 0 → 1 → 2 → 3: BREE 固定了每条臂对应的串口。请按 0 → 1 → 2 → 3 的顺序将机械臂接入主机：

3 · Camera rig3 · 相机机架

Three cameras, each with a fixed device index on the reference machine: 三台相机，参考主机上的设备编号固定如下：

Camera相机 Device设备 Mount安装位置
Global (overhead)全局（俯视）/dev/video0Fixed overhead mount over the workspace工作区上方固定俯视支架
Left wrist左腕/dev/video2Left follower gripper左从臂夹爪
Right wrist右腕/dev/video4Right follower gripper右从臂夹爪

All cameras run 640×480 @ 30 fps, MJPG. After mounting, their exact pose is locked in during System Calibration — mount them roughly now, align them precisely later. 所有相机以 640×480 @ 30 fps、MJPG 运行。安装后，相机的精确位姿会在系统标定阶段锁定——现在先大致安装，之后再精确对齐。

4 · AprilTag placement4 · AprilTag 摆放

  • Tag 0 (80 mm) is fixed in the global camera's view — flat, wrinkle-free, permanently attached to the workspace. 0 号标签（80 mm）固定在全局相机视野内——平整无褶皱，永久固定于工作区。
  • Tags 1 & 2 (50 mm) are used at calibration time to lock the wrist camera poses; place them in the wrist cameras' view during the check procedure. 1、2 号标签（50 mm）在校准时用于锁定腕部相机位姿；检查流程中将其置于腕部相机视野内。
  • Tags must be flat — a wrinkled or curved tag makes detection unreliable and will fail the tolerance gate. 标签必须平整——褶皱或弯曲会导致检测不可靠，并使容差判定失败。

Before the next step, ensure that:进入下一步之前，请确认：

  • All four arms power up and appear on ACM0–ACM3 in the assigned order.四条臂都能上电，并按分配顺序出现在 ACM0–ACM3。
  • All three cameras produce a live image at the expected indices (0 / 2 / 4).三台相机在预期编号（0 / 2 / 4）上都能出图。
  • AprilTags are printed at 100% scale, flat, and placed as described.AprilTag 按 100% 比例打印、平整，并按上述要求摆放。
  • Lighting and background are fixed — they must not change between sessions.光照与背景已固定——会话之间不得改变。

OVERVIEW

Core advantages

Why BREE

BREE democratizes bimanual research with its 600-dollar entry price, ensures scientific rigor through complete physical standardization, and accelerates data collection by turning distributed robot setups into a unified data ecosystem.

01

Low Cost

BREE delivers professional dual-arm manipulation capabilities for less than 600 dollars.

02

Reproducible

BREE eliminates hardware variance by standardizing every physical detail of the robot and its environment.

03

Scalable

BREE enables effortless data aggregation across multiple instances by ensuring perfect cross-platform compatibility.
